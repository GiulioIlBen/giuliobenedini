The word "copy" in the context of human work has been related to negative meaning. At school if you copy is wrong. When writing a gift card you should not copy from the internet. Your start up idea should be unique and "genial". But then you notice everybody is copying. Exams in high-school, gift cards with ChatGPT, startups main ideas. 

When coding, "copy and paste" could be a symptom that you are doing something wrong. It is not in the action of "copy" itself but in the "paste" action left alone. It should be coupled with the "think" action. Thinking is somewhat related to fatigue, that makes you slow down, compare, questioning. If you do so, than your gift card becomes more personal, when coping in school you will be learning and the startup value proposition becomes relevant.

When you have access to infinite information, you could assume that solutions have been already processed somewhere. Therefore, find and copy becomes a skill. Finally, it is important to give credit/attribution to others work when copy and pasting.

Find, copy, think, paste, credit: is all you need.
