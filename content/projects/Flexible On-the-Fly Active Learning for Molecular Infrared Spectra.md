Using active learning to train machine-learned potentials for ab initio-quality IR spectra.

## Objectives
- **Automated dataset generation**: the model picks its own training data.
- **Accurate and reliable IR spectra**: ab initio accuracy at a fraction of the cost.
- **Interpretation of IR spectra**: link spectra to molecules and peaks to molecular vibrations.

## Learning
- **Machine learning for chemistry**: hands-on experience with machine-learned interatomic potentials (MLIPs) and graph neural networks.
- **Software craftsmanship**: writing structured, maintainable code that others can build on ([repo](REPO_URL)), learned before AI coding assistants.
